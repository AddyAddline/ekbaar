## Judging criteria — six, unweighted
1. **Problem** — is this a real and important user problem?
2. **Working build** — does the main journey actually work?
3. **Usability** — simpler, clearer, more accessible?
4. **Product thinking** — are the choices thoughtful and well explained?
5. **End-to-end thinking** — does it address backend, infrastructure and *process*, not just interface?
6. **Honesty** — are limitations, mock data and dependencies clearly disclosed?

Criteria 4, 5 and 6 are **narrative**, not code. Most of the field will score near zero on 5 and 6. That is the opening.

## Deliverables
- **Live public browser link.** Opens with no access request. Mock consumer login credentials if login is required. Reviewers will **not** download a mobile app.
- **One video, max 2 minutes.** Minute 1 = demo as a citizen. Minute 2 = how you built it and why you made those choices. Both teammates may present.
- **Summary under 250 words.**
- Partner's registered email (both teammates cross-enter each other's).

## Hard rules
- **Codex is mandatory** for the submitted prototype and must be *meaningfully* involved in how it was built. Submission must explain how Codex contributed. Other tools/libraries allowed if disclosed.
- Every feature you demo must work. A Figma design is not enough. Static design is not enough.
- **Do not** access, test or interfere with a live government system. No reverse-engineering private systems or undocumented APIs. No scraping personal or restricted data.
- **No real** Aadhaar, PAN, passwords, OTPs, payment or health data. Synthetic/mock only.
- Must not appear official. No government logos implying approval. Label as an **independent hackathon prototype**.
- No resubmitting an old project. Disclose starter templates and OSS with licences.
- Solo or team of two, both 18+, both registered separately.
